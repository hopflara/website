Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Evidio collects, how we use it, and the third parties that process it on our behalf.

We only collect what the service needs to function, plus limited product analytics to improve the app (see below). We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking technologies, we do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Identity verification for commenting

Posting a comment on Evidio requires a one-time identity check. This keeps public discussion free of spam and impersonation.

This check is not active yet. Evidio does not currently collect government ID or biometric data of any kind. When the check launches, the ID document and selfie will be handled by a specialist identity-verification provider acting as our processor — Evidio will never receive or store the document image or the biometric data itself, only a pass/fail result, the legal name returned by the provider, and the date of the check. We will update this policy, name the provider, and ask for your explicit consent before any of that begins.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the service (accounts, posting, review, notifications).
  • To verify creator and expert credentials.
  • To keep the platform safe (moderation, reporting, blocking).
  • To run an automated AI fact-check on new submissions that gives expert reviewers an initial rating and notes. This assists human review; it does not replace it — every published post still requires expert approval.
  • To send you transactional messages, such as reviewer invitations and status updates about your submissions.
  • To understand how the app is used, through privacy-preserving product analytics. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not combine it with third-party data to track you across other apps or websites.

We rely on your consent and on the performance of our terms of service as the legal bases for this processing.

4. Data retention

We keep your account data and content for as long as your account is active. Content you post may remain visible to others until you delete it or your account is removed. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Your date of birth is kept alongside your account for as long as the account exists, so the age requirement stays enforceable, and is deleted with the rest of your data when you delete your account.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • To delete your account and associated data, use the “Delete account” option in the app (on your Account tab), or contact us at the address below.
  • For any other request, contact us at getevidio@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

6. Children

Evidio is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. You must be at least 16 to create an account. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and refuse registration if it shows you are under 16.

If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us at getevidio@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by email where appropriate.
