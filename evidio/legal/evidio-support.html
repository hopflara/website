Frequently asked questions

What are the roles on Evidio?

There are three: viewers browse, comment, and vote on approved content; creators can also submit posts with cited sources; and expert reviewers can also review submissions in their field. Creators and experts verify their credentials; viewers do not have to.

How do I become a verified creator or expert?

Pick the creator or expert role at sign-up (or add it later), then verify with one of: a published-paper DOI, a university email, or a proof-of-study PDF. Submit it from the verification screen. We review credentials within about 24 hours; meanwhile you can use the app as a viewer, and your creator/reviewer features unlock automatically once you’re approved.

I can’t see the Upload or Review tab.

Those tabs require a verified creator or expert account. Until your credentials are approved you have viewer permissions, so the tabs stay hidden. Once verified, they appear the next time your profile loads.

Why hasn’t my post been published yet?

Every post is checked before it goes live. First an automated AI fact-check gives reviewers an initial rating; then field-matched, credential-verified experts are invited, and the first three to accept review it. A post is published once it reaches 3 approvals, or rejected at 2 rejections. If there aren’t yet enough matching experts in its field, it waits in the queue rather than publishing.

How do I add sources to a post?

In the submit screen, add one or more sources, each with a title, a DOI link, and the claim it supports. A DOI looks like https://doi.org/10.1000/xyz (or 10.1000/xyz). Well-cited posts are far more likely to be approved.

My video upload is slow or stuck.

Uploading starts as soon as you pick a video, and a progress bar shows how far along it is — you can keep filling in the details meanwhile. Make sure you have a stable connection and keep the app open until it reaches 100%. If it fails, tap “Change video” and pick it again.

How do I report content or block someone?

Open the … menu on any post (it’s on the feed card and the post screen), or long-press a comment, and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Blocking hides that person’s posts and comments from you everywhere; you can unblock from your Account tab.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Account tab and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your data (posts, comments, and votes) and cannot be undone.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap the password-reset link and enter your email. We’ll send a secure link to set a new password.

Comments aren’t loading or posting.

Make sure you’re on the latest version of the app and have a working connection, then reopen the post. If a message appears when you try to comment, it will describe the problem — send it to us and we’ll help.

Does Evidio show ads or track me?

No. Evidio shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, and does no cross-app tracking. We use privacy-preserving product analytics tied only to your account identifier — see the Privacy Policy.

Accounts & roles

You sign in with your email and password. At sign-up you choose how you want to take part:

  • Viewer — comment on and follow verified content on the feed. No credential verification needed.
  • Creator — everything a viewer can do, plus submit posts (text, image, or video) with cited sources.
  • Expert reviewer — everything above, plus review submissions in your field of expertise.

You can hold more than one role. Creators and experts need a field of expertise on their profile so submissions can be matched to the right reviewers.

Getting verified

Creator and expert features unlock once your academic credentials are verified. From the verification screen, choose a method and submit it:

  • Published paper (DOI) — paste a doi.org link to a paper you authored.
  • University email — verify with your institutional email address.
  • Proof of study (PDF) — upload an enrolment letter or transcript.

After you submit, you’ll see a confirmation and can continue into the app as a viewer. We review credentials within about 24 hours; you don’t have to do anything — your reviewer or creator rights are granted automatically once we verify them. Verification documents are kept private and are visible only to the Evidio admin.

Submitting a post

Creators submit from the Upload tab. Choose a content type — text, image, or video — add a title and (for text) your content, pick a topic, and add your sources.

  • Sources are what set Evidio apart: add each as a title, a DOI link, and the claim it supports.
  • Images: attach up to 10 from your photo library.
  • Video: uploading begins the moment you pick the file, with a progress bar; submit finishes the last bit.

When you submit, your post enters review with the status “pending”. You’ll be notified when it’s decided, and you can see your submissions and their status on your Account tab.

How review works

Evidio publishes nothing until it has been checked. The pipeline is:

  • Automated AI fact-check — a new submission is checked automatically, giving reviewers an initial rating and notes. This assists reviewers; it never publishes anything on its own.
  • Expert invitations — credential-verified experts in the post’s field (or an adjacent field) are invited; the first three to accept become its reviewers. Creators can never review their own post.
  • Decision — 3 approvals publish the post to the feed; 2 rejections reject it, with reviewer feedback to the creator.

Approval means three experts found the post sound enough to publish — it is not a guarantee of scientific accuracy, and content on Evidio is not professional advice. Always evaluate the cited sources yourself.

Reporting & blocking

Evidio does not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behavior. You have two tools, on every post:

  • Report — open the … menu on a post (or long-press a comment), choose Report, and pick a reason. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and content or users that break the rules are removed.
  • Block — from the same menu, block a user to hide their posts and comments from you everywhere they appear. Manage or undo blocks from your Account tab.

Managing your data

You stay in control of your information:

  • Edit your profile — update your name, bio, and field from the Account tab.
  • Delete your account — Account tab → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data (posts, comments, votes) and cannot be undone.
  • Privacy — what we collect and who processes it is described in the Privacy Policy; your rights and our terms are in the Terms of Use.
