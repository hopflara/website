1. Introduction & scope

Brainlist (store name Brainlist: Plan Your Day) is an iPhone productivity and planning app from Hopflara that brings your calendar, tasks, quick notes, and habits into one calm “Greenhouse at dawn” interface. This Privacy Policy describes how the app handles your information. It applies to the Brainlist iOS app (bundle ID com.hopflara.brainlist), which is iPhone-only, portrait-only, and requires iOS 18 or later.

Brainlist is designed to keep your information close to you. Your content is stored on your device using Apple’s SwiftData and, when iCloud is available, mirrored to your own private iCloud/CloudKit database so it syncs across your devices. It is held in your iCloud account, not on Hopflara’s servers. Signing in with Apple is required to use the app. Optional AI assistance is available to Brainlist Pro subscribers and is processed by a third-party cloud AI provider acting as our processor; free users get equivalent on-device suggestions with nothing leaving the device. Brainlist shows no ads and performs no cross-app tracking.

By using Brainlist, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

What we do not collect

  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK is present in the app’s code.
  • No precise or GPS location is collected. The event ‘location’ field is plain text you type, and the app does not access Core Location.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, or health/fitness data are accessed.
  • No personal data is sold to third parties.
  • No user identity (name, email, account ID, or device ID) is sent to our third-party cloud AI provider with AI requests.
  • No payment-card details are handled or stored by the app. Apple processes all payments.

  • When AI is off or you are offline, no event/task text is sent anywhere for AI; suggestions are generated on your device.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above only to operate and improve the app and to provide the features you choose to use, including:

  • Run core features: the Today agenda, Calendar, Tasks (List, Matrix, and Wheel), rich task details, events, Brain Dump, Focus sessions, the Garden, Templates / Macros, and (for Pro) plant care.
  • Sync your content across your devices through your private iCloud/CloudKit database when iCloud is available.
  • Send reminders and notifications you set up for events, tasks, and plant care, delivered as local notifications.
  • Calculate insights and gamification on your device: peak productive hour, weekly completion chart and trend, points, levels, daily streaks, the growing tree, and the mascot’s mood.
  • Generate optional AI suggestions for Brainlist Pro (smart event times, task breakdowns, companion reminders, and coaching tips), or equivalent on-device suggestions for everyone else.
  • Provide and manage your Brainlist Pro subscription and restore your purchases through Apple.
  • Sign you in using Sign in with Apple.
  • Maintain reliability and respond to support, including diagnosing sync and connectivity issues.

Where a legal basis is required (for example under the EU/UK GDPR), we generally rely on performing our agreement with you (providing the app you requested), your consent (for optional permissions and AI features), and our legitimate interests in keeping the app secure and reliable.

4. Your content & where it is stored

Your content (events, tasks, notes, plants, productivity stats, and settings) is stored on your device using Apple’s SwiftData. When you are signed into iCloud, that data is mirrored to your private iCloud/CloudKit database and synced across the devices that use the same Apple ID. This database is hosted by Apple inside your own iCloud account; it is not stored on Hopflara’s servers.

If iCloud is unavailable (for example, there is no iCloud account, the container is not provisioned, or there is an entitlement mismatch), the app automatically falls back to an on-device, local-only store and remains fully functional offline. Sync resumes when iCloud becomes available again. Cross-device iCloud sync and .ics import/export are available to all signed-in users, not only Brainlist Pro.

Signing in with Apple is required to use the app; the sign-in is handled natively by Apple and Brainlist keeps only your Apple user identifier and display name on your device. Subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store. AI requests are sent to our third-party cloud AI provider only when AI features are enabled (Pro); otherwise processing stays on your device. Calendar import reads upcoming events from your device’s calendars (today onward, including accounts you have linked in iOS such as Google or Exchange), and you can export your events as an .ics file through the iOS share sheet.

5. AI features

Brainlist’s AI features are optional and are designed to work without sending your data off the device whenever possible. Server-backed AI requires a Brainlist Pro subscription with a configured AI service. We intentionally keep the specific AI provider and model unbranded to users; we refer to it here as our third-party cloud AI provider, which acts as our data processor.

When AI is enabled, only the minimum text needed for the requested suggestion is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and the provider returns a suggestion:

  • Smart-time and companion-reminder suggestions: the event title.
  • Subtask breakdown: the task title and details.
  • Personalised coaching tip: only aggregate productivity stats (your peak hour, weekly completed count, and a short trend word), never your task or event text.

Inputs are truncated before sending (titles to 200 characters, details to 1000 characters), and no name, email, account ID, or device identifier is attached, so AI requests are not tied to your identity. Requests are sent to a Cloudflare Worker operated by Hopflara, where they are processed by Cloudflare Workers AI (currently the Kimi K2.6 model) acting as our processor — used only to provide the feature and return the result to you. The request bodies are not stored in an application database, although Cloudflare-side and platform logging or retention may apply and is governed by Cloudflare.

If you are a free user, are offline, or the AI service is unreachable, the app automatically falls back to equivalent on-device suggestions (keyword heuristics and locally computed tips) and no text is transmitted for AI.

AI outputs are automated, may be inaccurate or incomplete, and are not professional advice. Please review them before relying on them.

6. Device permissions

Brainlist asks for iOS permissions only to power specific features, and you can grant or revoke them at any time in iOS Settings. Declining a permission disables the related feature but leaves the rest of the app working.

  • Microphone: capture voice notes (dictation) in Brain Dump.
  • Speech Recognition: transcribe your dictation into note text using Apple Speech.
  • Calendars (Full Access): import events from your device calendars so they appear alongside your tasks.
  • Notifications: send local reminders for events, tasks, and plant care.
  • iCloud / CloudKit: sync your data across your devices via your signed-in iCloud account (granted through iCloud; no separate in-app prompt).
  • Siri & Shortcuts: add a note to Brainlist by voice without opening the app.

Brainlist relies on a small number of service providers to deliver sign-in, sync, payments, dictation, notifications, and optional AI. Each provider receives only the data needed for the feature you use, and handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Apple

Data involved: your name and email at sign-in (Sign in with Apple); your content synced to your own private iCloud/CloudKit database; subscription, purchase, and restore data; dictation audio for transcription; and notification device tokens. Apple Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare (AI processing)

Used for: processing the text you submit through AI features (smart times, companion reminders, task breakdowns, and coaching tips) and returning suggestions. Requests go to a Cloudflare Worker operated by Hopflara and are processed by Cloudflare Workers AI (currently the Kimi K2.6 model), acting as our processor.

Data involved: event and task titles, task details, and aggregate productivity stats (peak hour, weekly completed count, trend), only when AI features are enabled (Pro). No name, email, account ID, or device identifier is included. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

8. No advertising or tracking

Brainlist does not show ads. It contains no advertising SDK, does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not use SKAdNetwork, and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. Because the app does not track you, it shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Your information is never sold or shared for advertising purposes.

10. Data retention & deletion

Your data remains available as long as you keep it. When you delete an event, task, note, or plant, it is moved to Recently Deleted (a soft delete) and kept for 30 days so you can restore it; after 30 days it is permanently removed, or you can permanently delete it sooner from the Trash (Settings > Data > Recently Deleted).

Data synced to iCloud stays in your private iCloud/CloudKit database until you delete it in the app or remove it from your iCloud account; data also remains on your device until deleted. Our third-party cloud AI provider does not store AI request bodies in its own database, but platform/provider logging and retention may apply to submitted text and is governed by that provider as our processor.

Apple (authentication, iCloud, purchases, dictation) retains the data it processes under its own privacy policy.

To delete your data or remove account/identity data: delete items in the app and permanently remove them from Recently Deleted, sign out from Settings, and remove synced copies from iCloud (Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage). For removal of identity data held by sign-in or other providers, contact Hopflara via the support contact on the App Store listing.

11. Security

Network requests for AI features are made over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data synced to iCloud is stored in your private iCloud/CloudKit database, protected by your Apple ID and Apple’s infrastructure; data on your device is protected by iOS device security. Payments are handled entirely by Apple, so the app never sees or stores your payment-card details, and sign-in is delegated to Apple (Sign in with Apple) so the app never sees or stores a password. AI requests carry no account, name, email, or device identifier.

No security method is perfect. Please help protect your information by keeping your device passcode and Apple ID secure.

12. Children’s privacy

Brainlist is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). Hopflara does not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact Hopflara through the support contact on the app’s App Store listing so it can be removed.

Sign-in is required, and account creation should be completed by someone of the appropriate age; an adult should set up and manage the app for younger users where permitted.

13. Your rights & choices

You stay in control of your information. Depending on where you live, some of these may be legal rights; all of them are choices Brainlist gives you:

  • Access and export your data: export your events as a standard .ics file via the iOS share sheet.
  • Correct or update your information: edit any event, task, note, or plant directly in the app.
  • Delete your data: move items to Recently Deleted and permanently delete them, and remove synced data from your iCloud account (Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage).
  • Opt out of AI: AI is optional; using the free/offline experience keeps all processing on your device with nothing sent to the AI provider.
  • Control device permissions: grant or revoke microphone, speech recognition, calendar, and notification access anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Manage your subscription and restore purchases through your Apple ID.
  • Sign out at any time from Settings.
  • Make a legal request: where applicable by law, request access to or deletion of personal data held by Hopflara or its processors via the support contact on the App Store listing.

14. International users

Brainlist relies on global service providers (Apple and our third-party cloud AI provider) that may process and store data in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States. When you use a feature that involves one of these providers, your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations under the safeguards in their respective privacy policies.

Your privacy rights may vary by region (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act). Where such laws apply, you can exercise your applicable rights using the choices in this policy or by contacting Hopflara through the support contact on the App Store listing.
