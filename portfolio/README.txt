Portfolio assets for social.html (your UGC creator page).
Upload files here on GitHub with these EXACT names, and they replace the
pink placeholder boxes automatically (refresh after uploading).

PHOTOS (this folder, portfolio/):
  hero.jpg          -> your main hero photo (portrait, appears in the arch)
  intro-poster.jpg  -> a thumbnail image shown before the intro video plays (optional)
  service-1.jpg     -> first photo in the Services collage (portrait)
  service-2.jpg     -> second photo in the Services collage (portrait)

INTRO VIDEO (this folder, portfolio/):
  intro.mp4         -> your "Meet the Creator" intro video

EXAMPLE VIDEOS (portfolio/videos/):
  video-1.mp4 ... video-6.mp4  -> the six Ad-or-Organic example videos
                                  (vertical 9:16 works best)

Tip: keep videos small (a few MB each) so the page loads fast. MP4 (H.264)
plays everywhere. You can also edit the captions, the "AD OR ORGANIC" labels,
the email, and the social usernames directly in social.html.
